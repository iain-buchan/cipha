# Small Area Vulnerability Index (SAVI)

**Paper.** Daras K, Alexiou A, Rose TC, Buchan I, Taylor-Robinson D, Barr B. How does vulnerability to COVID-19 vary between communities in England? Developing a Small Area Vulnerability Index (SAVI). *Journal of Epidemiology and Community Health* 2021;75:729-734. https://doi.org/10.1136/jech-2020-215227 (preprint: SSRN 3650050)

**What it is.** An empirically informed index of COVID-19 vulnerability for every Middle Super Output Area (MSOA) in England, built from a multivariable Poisson model of COVID-19 mortality on area characteristics (model selection by AIC), published in June 2020 to help local authorities target their response.

## Open data

The index is published on the Place-based Longitudinal Data Resource (PLDR) under the Open Government Licence v3, dataset DOI https://doi.org/10.17638/datacat.liverpool.ac.uk/1172, authors Konstantinos Daras and Benjamin Barr:

- https://pldr.org/dataset/e6kl0: SAVI v1.00 MSOA data (CSV, 1.9 MB), MSOA boundaries (shapefile, 10.8 MB) and the indicator specification (PDF)

To hold a copy here, download the CSV and specification PDF into this folder and cite as: Daras K, Barr B (2021). Small Area Vulnerability Index (SAVI). PLDR. https://doi.org/10.17638/datacat.liverpool.ac.uk/1172. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
